QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Solar essentials.

What is an On-Grid Solar System?

An On-Grid system connects to the utility grid. It can reduce electricity purchased from the grid when solar generation is available; export and billing rules depend on local utility policy.

What is a Hybrid Solar System?

A Hybrid system combines solar generation with grid power and battery storage, depending on the equipment selected.

Does solar work during cloudy or rainy weather?

Solar panels can still generate electricity in cloudy conditions, but output is generally lower than in strong direct sunlight.

How much electricity can a solar system generate?

Generation depends on system capacity, sunlight, shading, orientation, weather and equipment. A site assessment helps estimate expected output.

How much roof space is required?

It depends on system capacity, panel dimensions, roof shape, shading and access requirements. Ask for a site assessment for a more useful estimate.

What maintenance does a solar system need?

Typical care includes keeping panels reasonably clean, checking for visible damage and arranging periodic electrical and performance inspections.

Does S.K Bright provide installation and service?

S.K Bright Solar Energy offers solar installation, service and maintenance enquiries. Contact the team to confirm availability for your location and system.

Which areas does S.K Bright serve?

The service area is Uttar Pradesh, with the company based in Ramnagar, Varanasi. Contact us to confirm service availability for your specific location.
